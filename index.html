Filippo Teoldi

Filippo is a visual journalist at The Economist, working with data, charts, maps and interactive stories.

He joined The Economist in 2023, after working as a data editor at Domani and as a junior economist at the Economic Advisory Council to the Italian Prime Minister. He studied economics and political science at Bocconi and Columbia. Away from the screen he enjoys biographies, photography and dahlias, and one day hopes to move to Japan.
