Filippo Teoldi

Filippo is a London-based visual journalist. He works with data, charts, maps, and everything that helps visualize the world we live in. He has a background in economics and holds degrees from Columbia University and Bocconi University. He currently works at The Economist as an interactive visual journalist.

He enjoys biographies, photography, and dahlias - and struggles with the idea that we only live one life.
One day, he hopes to move to Japan.
